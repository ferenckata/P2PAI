Prototype to Product

Integrating AI into complex systems responsibly.

The P2P-AI initiative aims to develop a socio-technical, startup-inspired Agile framework for responsible and sustainable AI adoption.

Why this matters

Integrating AI research prototypes into existing complex systems can improve efficiency, decision support, and user experiences, but it requires more than a technically capable model.

  • Critical requirements are addressed throughout product development.
  • Stakeholder trust, legal constraints, and operational feasibility matter.
  • Teams can reduce technical, architectural, and process debt.

Background & context

A practical bridge from prototype to product

The project addresses the transition from a working prototype to an integrated AI module, with attention to both technical and organizational conditions. A central challenge is ensuring that critical requirements — such as stakeholder needs, trust, legal constraints, and operational feasibility — are addressed throughout product development rather than treated as late-stage additions.

The proposed P2P-AI framework will help organizations make informed trade-offs, avoid technical, architectural, and process debt, and improve the predictability of costs, benefits, and outcomes across AI integration initiatives.

Stakeholder involvement is particularly important, since users need to understand and trust the resulting AI tool for it to create value.

Objectives

What the initiative is designed to achieve

1. Framework for transition

Develop a P2P-AI framework for moving from research-grade AI prototypes to well-integrated AI modules in large, existing systems.

2. Trade-off management

Support explicit trade-off management among qualities such as maintainability, accuracy, scalability, security, and user experience.

3. Domain and governance expertise

Incorporate domain, legal, and philosophical expertise at appropriate points in an iterative development process and define success criteria and evaluation methods.
