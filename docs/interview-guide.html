Interview guide

Research questions for stakeholder interviews

The following questions are intended to explore organizational experience, legal readiness, team dynamics, and the practical realities of working with AI in complex systems.

Understanding use cases

Through interviews and workshops, we aim to understand the challenges organizations face around product quality, legal compliance, and agentic coding, as well as the effects on team dynamics and ownership.

The goal is to explore how teams experience the transition from a working prototype to a well-integrated AI module in large, existing systems, and what conditions make that transition sustainable and trustworthy.

Core interview questions

  • What challenges did your organization encounter with the quality of its products beyond technical difficulties?
  • What were your experiences with following the law? Was it simple enough, and did you have sufficient knowledge and resources to create a good-enough product?
  • What challenges did you encounter with agentic coding? How were ownership and team dynamics affected?
  • How did the organization balance product quality, oversight, and delivery pressure when introducing AI-based assistance or automation into existing systems?
  • Which requirements were most difficult to operationalize in practice: trust, legal constraints, maintainability, accountability, or user experience?
  • What kinds of trade-offs did your team have to make between speed, cost, quality, and governance?
  • How did stakeholders understand and trust the resulting AI tool, and what was required to build that trust over time?
  • What activities or structures helped or hindered successful transitions from research prototype to product integration?

Suggested discussion themes

  • Product quality and operational challenges
  • Legal and compliance experience
  • Agentic coding and team responsibility
  • Trust, usability, and stakeholder adoption
  • Trade-off management in AI integration
